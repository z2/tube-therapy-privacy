Tube Therapy — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026 · Publisher: Arklouder

Short version: Tube Therapy works entirely inside your browser. It does not track you, does not collect your browsing or viewing history, and does not sell anything about you. Data leaves your browser only when you choose to send a wish or make an optional payment.

1. What the extension does

Tube Therapy changes how youtube.com looks in your browser: it hides Shorts, members-only, watched, live and other distracting items, keeps end-screen cards off the video, and switches videos to their original audio track and title. It runs only on youtube.com pages (and on extensionpay.com payment pages, see section 4).

2. Data stored on your device

  • Your settings (which features are on, end-screen mode, interface language) are saved with Chrome's built-in storage. If Chrome Sync is on, Chrome syncs them across your own devices. We never receive them.
  • Supporter status (whether you made an optional payment) is cached locally so the badge shows even offline.

3. Data you choose to send: the Wish list

If you send a wish from the extension menu, we receive only:

  • the text you typed;
  • your email address, only if you entered one (so we can reply);
  • your browser's interface language and the extension version.

Wishes are stored in a private Google Sheet owned by the publisher and are used only to read your feedback, improve the extension and reply to you. You can ask us to delete a wish at any time (see section 7).

4. Optional payments ("Buy me a house")

All features are free. If you choose to make a one-time support payment, it is handled by ExtensionPay and processed by Stripe. The extension never sees or stores your card details. ExtensionPay keeps your email address and payment status so you can restore your supporter badge on another device. Their own privacy policies apply to that data.

5. Requests to YouTube

To show original video titles, the extension asks YouTube's public oEmbed service (youtube.com/oembed) for the title of videos you are looking at. These requests go to YouTube only, as part of your normal YouTube browsing; no data is sent to us.

6. What we do not do

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, ads or third-party trackers.
  • We do not collect your browsing history, YouTube history, searches or account data.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data, and we do not use it for credit or lending decisions.
  • The extension does not load or run remote code.

7. Your choices and contact

You can change or reset all settings in the extension menu, and uninstalling the extension removes the data stored on your device. To ask about or delete a wish you sent, send us a message through the Wish list in the extension, or use the contact email shown on the Tube Therapy page in the Chrome Web Store.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
